# Catalogue and assets

Everything perception is measured on is fictional. `tools/label_factory.py` generates the product
catalogue deterministically from a seed: names and brands are invented, labels are original artwork,
and every barcode is a valid EAN-13 with a `2000000...` prefix (GS1's restricted-circulation range),
so no real product is ever matched.

![One front-panel view of each of the 20 catalogue products, GEN-001 to GEN-020](/img/perception/catalogue.png)

## Products

| SKUs | Shape | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GEN-001` to `GEN-004` | can (radius 0.033 m, height 0.122 m) | two look-alike pairs |
| `GEN-005` to `GEN-008` | small bottle | two look-alike pairs |
| `GEN-009` to `GEN-012` | large bottle | two look-alike pairs |
| `GEN-013` to `GEN-016` | contoured bottle | two look-alike pairs; waisted, ribbed visual, perception-only |
| `GEN-017`, `GEN-018` | 200 mL carton, 65 x 40 x 105 mm | a look-alike pair |
| `GEN-019` | 1 L carton, 95 x 60 x 165 mm | |
| `GEN-020` | slim 330 mL box, 55 x 25 x 150 mm | **no barcode**: its back panel carries none and its manifest row has no `gtin` |

A look-alike pair shares brand and kind and differs in the flavour word, with the same layout and
colours. That is the case an embedding cannot resolve and label text can. The same idea is a
scenario of its own: `same_shape_products.yaml` stocks two cans of identical dimensions whose labels
differ in one word, and `same_shape_lanes.yaml` makes `lane_04` expect the citrus can, so reading the
word right fills both lanes and reading it wrong leaves selection with no eligible pair.

## Label textures

Every label is a full unwrapped strip with a front panel (brand, flavour, a logo of random shapes)
and a back panel (ingredients, nutrition table, disclaimers, a valid EAN-13).

![Unwrapped label strip of a can: front panel left, back panel with barcode right](/img/perception/label-can.webp)

- **Round products** use one strip. Gazebo maps it once around the circumference and along the axis.
- **Boxes** use a **face atlas**: the unfolded net at 8000 px/m with front, right, back and left in one
  row and top and bottom below. The manifest row carries the face table (pixel rectangle, outward
  normal and the product-frame directions of the image's right and up edges, always right-handed so no
  face is mirrored), plus `box_variant`, `visual_mesh`, `size_xyz_m`, `mass_kg` and `atlas_px`.
  `tools/generate_box_meshes.py` writes one OBJ per variant with hard edges and per-face UVs from the
  same layout function the atlas uses.

![Face atlas of the 1 L carton: front, right, back and left in a row, top and bottom below](/img/perception/label-box.webp)

**Textures are stored upright.** Every `gen-NNN.png` reads correctly as stored. Gazebo unwraps a
cylinder primitive with the strip rotated by half a turn, while the contoured bottle's own UVs show it
upright. Each manifest row therefore carries `texture_rotation_deg`: 180 for `can`, `small_bottle` and
`large_bottle`, 0 for `contour_bottle` and for boxes. The launch file installs the texture through
`restocker_gazebo/label_textures.py`, the only place the half-turn is applied: a rotation of 180 loads a
pixel-exact rotated copy cached under the system temporary directory, anything else loads unchanged. The
registry's reference-view renderer maps the strip as stored and applies no rotation.

## Geometry and physics

- The contoured bottle has a waisted, ribbed visual (`meshes/contour_bottle.obj`) while its collision is
  the plain cylinder of radius 0.036 m. Perception sees a bottle shape; grasping, friction and lane
  geometry are those of a cylinder.
- A box has no class fallback. Its collision and inertia come from `box_size`, and a box lies on its back
  (front face up) in a lane column, packed by its depth.
- The visual is named `product_body_visual` and the collision `product_body_collision`; sdformat merges
  a material or friction extension only into elements whose generated name contains the merging
  pattern, and drops it silently otherwise.

![Gazebo renders of the carton products: upright, from the side, lying on a face, and the back panel](/img/perception/gazebo-boxes.webp)

## What is simulator-only

- The catalogue, its labels and its registry views exist only in the simulator. Registry views of the
  simulated products are rendered offline from the same textures the simulator shows, so they measure
  that the renderer and the simulator agree, not that real cartons are identified.
- `generated_catalogue_products.yaml` lays all 20 SKUs on a tray. It is a scene for looking at
  labels, not a restocking scenario.
- The simulator's depth camera is ideal. Noise in the benchmarks is a synthetic model applied to the
  render; see [Benchmarks](./benchmarks).
- The simulated attachment, gripper and lane physics apply to cylinders. Boxes are observed, not
  manipulated; see [Known boundaries](/docs/perception/#known-boundaries).
