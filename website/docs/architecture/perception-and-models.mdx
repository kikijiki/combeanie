# Perception and models

Perception supplies **untrusted observations** that world state validates; wrist product/lane
perception and overhead obstacle evidence are required on the sensor-driven default path.
The recovery reasoner is optional and advisory. Neither can emit joint commands, unchecked
trajectories, or inventory commits.

## Observation topics

| Topic (defaults) | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `/perception/object_observations` | Sole camera producer → world state (wrist confirm duty on the default path; the overhead pipeline only with `perception:=true`) |
| `/perception/ground_truth/object_observations` | GT when the camera owns the ingest topic |
| `/perception/tray_candidates` | Tray **overview** hypotheses only — never ingested by the world state |
| `/perception/lane_observations` | Camera lane survey |
| `/perception/lane_stock` | Lane stock estimate: count, composition, cannot-tell (`lane_stock:=true`; nothing consumes it) |
| `/perception/inventory`, `/query_inventory` | Aggregate stock view (`inventory:=true`; nothing consumes it) |
| `/perception/ground_truth/lane_observations` | GT / evaluator |
| `/perception/obstacle_observations` | Depth obstacles → projector |

Ground truth publishes on `/perception/ground_truth/object_observations`, and the wrist confirm duty
owns the object ingest topic (`/perception/object_observations`), which the world state admits. The
world state pins exactly one publisher per ingest topic, so running a different producer there (for
example the overhead `perception:=true` pipeline) means turning the confirm duty off.

**The overhead camera is for obstacle detection only.** Its depth stream feeds
`depth_obstacle_node` (`obstacle_perception`, on by default) and the projector requires that
evidence before certifying the scene; its *object* pipeline (`perception:=true`) is **off** on
the default path, so no default-path product pose comes from the overhead camera.

### The two-tier tray survey

The wrist tray duties split their outputs on purpose. `tray_overview_perception:=true` runs the
overview duty, which publishes **candidates** on `/perception/tray_candidates` — hypotheses with
no world identity, invisible to the world state, so no transfer can be planned from overview
evidence alone. `tray_confirm_perception:=true` runs the confirm duty on the ingest topic; only
its close-range, admissible-confirmation observations become world evidence.

The `survey_tray` action (`tray_survey:=true`, which also starts `survey_viewpoint`) is the
survey coordinator (it commands arm motion but does not commit inventory): it visits the
overview stations through `/survey_viewpoint`, selects one candidate, solves an admissible confirmation viewpoint, and answers `OUTCOME_CONFIRMED` with the
confirmed observation or a typed `OUTCOME_REFUTED` (`CLASS_MISMATCH`, `SKU_MISMATCH`,
`ABSENT`). Cancellation abandons unvisited stations and retains collected candidates. A cancellation
request alone is not proof that arm motion stopped; inspect terminal motion evidence before
issuing another behavior. Timeout and late-result reconciliation are limitations.

The shipped camera backend is classical (`ColourDepthBackend`), not a neural net. Any learned
pose or detection backend sits behind the same message contracts.

### Measured accuracy of the confirm duty

On seeded trays, against simulator ground truth, the confirm duty's translation error at confirmation
range is 0.19 to 0.36 mm over 12 admitted confirmations, with the world-Y residual at most 0.27 mm.
All 12 are within the covariance-derived attachment budget and none fails closed. The overview duty's
median translation error is 0.69 mm.

The published axis is an algebraic circle fit to the convex hull of the top-face band's silhouette. A
plain band mean would be pulled 2 to 7 mm toward the camera at the shipped 51-degree tray elevation.
The confirm duty's `translation_sigma_floor_m` is 0.002 m, which gives a fidelity budget of
**3 × 2 + 1 = 7 mm before the per-product jaw ceiling (17, 16 and 5 mm for can, small and large)**.

Overview does not separate touching same-class products. Close-neighbour and non-upright cells are
refused rather than admitted with a wrong pose. The tray duties are **on** by default
(`tray_overview_perception` and `tray_confirm_perception` are the sensor-driven default's product
source); the overhead object pipeline is off (`perception:=false`).

### Naming on the default path

The confirm duty measures where a product is; its name comes from the stocking manifest. A category
stocked once names any well-formed detection of that category. A category stocked more than once is
named by position: a detection takes an instance's name only if that instance's reference position is
within 0.035 m and every other instance is at least 0.020 m farther away. Otherwise the detection is
dropped. Naming a product from its label is the job of the identity channel, described in
[Registry and identity](/docs/perception/registry-and-identity).

## Obstacle boxes

`obstacle_perception` (default **on**) starts `depth_obstacle_node` and makes the projector
require obstacle evidence; both additionally gate on `cameras`, so a cameraless composition
never demands evidence nothing can produce. Stale boxes are retained under degradation. This is
the overhead camera's role on the default path: obstacle detection only, with its object
perception off.

## Advisory reasoner

`reasoner_enabled:=false` by default. When enabled, the reasoner may only select among
enumerated recovery primitives and may not escalate aggressiveness past deterministic policy.
It uses a private mailbox, because sharing the coordinator inbox would turn inbox pressure
into a safety fault.
